# Research gap and bounded contribution

The project's documented candidate gap concerns sensitivity of 24-month progression claims to label construction, prediction anchoring, resampling unit and calibration, and whether temporal/multimodal representations add information on comparable evaluation populations. This is a research motivation, not a verified novelty claim.

ADNI-to-NACC transport, conventional prediction, multimodal models and sequence models already have substantial prior work. Merely implementing more classifiers or reporting a larger score is insufficient. A defensible study needs a precisely matched comparator, explicit prediction time, independent confirmation, and diagnosis-linked predictor sensitivities. The early-onset question also needs actual onset information rather than enrollment age alone.

The workspace contains multiple literature reviews and pivots. They are historical records, not a completed current systematic search. A refreshed, documented primary-source search and methodological comparison remains required before any priority or novelty claim.
