# Research overview

YOAD began with a younger-onset Alzheimer's disease interest and expanded into methodological investigation of 24-month dementia progression among people with MCI. ADNI was investigated for development, and NACC for potential independent validation. Their roles, mappings and access decisions evolved; they are not assumed pooled or interchangeable.

The source contains calendar-based and visit-code outcome construction, snapshot predictors, repeated visit anchors, conventional models, multimodal fusion and temporal neural candidates. The research question is whether representation and outcome choices change discrimination, calibration and apparent modality value when participants and evaluation targets are held comparable. This release makes the software inspectable while withholding unverified scientific performance claims.

Earlier-onset questions require reliable onset ages and adequate events. Enrollment-age proxies answer a different question. This repository does not establish an onset-defined YOAD cohort or a clinically effective early-onset predictor.
